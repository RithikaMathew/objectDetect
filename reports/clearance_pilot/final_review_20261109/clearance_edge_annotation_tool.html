Passing-clearance edge review

This page is an optional manual check. Click the image at the nearest visible edge, assign the click to the cyclist or car, and download the labels. It produces pixel gaps, not metres.

What this is for: this creates a manual pixel-level record of the gap between the cyclist and car. It is not required for the existing exploratory detector calculation. It is useful only if you want a human-reviewed comparison, or later obtain a lateral calibration that can convert pixels to metres.
How to review: click the image at the cyclist's side facing the car, then click the button to assign it. Do the same for the car's side facing the cyclist. Occlusion means one object blocks part of the other or hides the edge. Frame usable means both relevant edges are visible enough to label; choose No when they are hidden, blurry, or there is no car.
