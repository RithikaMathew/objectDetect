We completed a small video-only pilot using three 30-second Colonial clips. Two detector settings and 12 trajectory/geometry scenarios compared passing clearance estimates, minimum separation, TTC and PET, including Kalman filtering and RTS smoothing. Visual review of selected sequences identified two actual same-direction car passes and one false car detection on a bicycle wheel, which caused the sole zero-clearance/TTC/PET alarm. Earlier manually clicked cyclist trajectories were used for a same-window speed comparison, with shared calibration uncertainty retained. All cyclist positions fall outside the saved car-calibration support region, and the current files do not provide a verified lateral scale or sufficiently reliable vehicle-side footprints for these passes. Therefore, no validated physical passing gap or near-miss rate is reported from this pilot. The packet preserves the reviewed passing-event evidence, rejected false detection, original model estimates and their limitations.
