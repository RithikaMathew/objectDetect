Why the alongside gate fails
Assumed footprints are centered on projected detector bottom-centres. Learned coordinate units are unvalidated, not defensible metres. A rejected physical metric does not erase the reviewed pass.


Review checklist
  • Check real identities and detector namespaces.
  • Use one-based frames and PTS; retain absolute-clock uncertainty.
  • Distinguish visible contacts, detector proxies, manual clicks and reconstructed positions.
  • Check path/barrier/road-space relationship and occlusion.
  • For PET, support a shared physical zone and each occupancy boundary independently.
  • Keep screening separate from reviewed labels. Do not infer braking from noisy pixel derivatives.
  • Save only needed new annotations; six existing edge frames are already loaded.
